Axial Spine and Ribs — Screening and Diagnosis
COMMUNICATION: Appropriately communicates to patient about the physical exam being done (uses lay terms, speaks to pt, assures understanding). 1
Obtains consent before doing the exam AND again with any particularly sensitive parts of the exam. 2
WASHES HANDS: Prior to technique (may do at beginning of encounter) – hand sanitizer, or at least 20s with soap and water (if student is cued to do this task, then no credit will be given). 3
HEAD/CERVICAL SPINE SCREENING: Effectively segmentally screens the entire cervical spine (OA-C7) with appropriate contact and force vector bilaterally. 4
HEAD/CERVICAL SPINE SEGMENTAL DIAGNOSIS: Correctly performs a 3-positional segmental diagnosis of the OA, AA, and most restricted lower cervical (C2-C7) segment AND is accurate with the diagnosis. 5
THORACIC SPINE SCREENING: Effectively segmentally screens the entire thoracic spine (T1-T12) AND the thoracic outlet with appropriate contact and force vector bilaterally. 6
THORACIC SPINE SEGMENTAL DIAGNOSIS: Correctly performs a 3-positional segmental diagnosis of the most restricted thoracic (T1-T12) segment AND the thoracic outlet AND is accurate with the diagnosis. 7
RIB CAGE SCREENING: Effectively screens the entire rib cage (ribs 1-12) for respiratory (inhaled/exhaled) AND mechanical (anterior/posterior) dysfunctions with appropriate contact and force vector bilaterally. 8
RIB CAGE DIAGNOSIS: Correctly performs a positional segmental diagnosis of the most restricted ribs AND is accurate with the diagnosis. 9
