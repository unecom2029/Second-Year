Mesenteric ischemia

  • Elderly + AF + pain out of proportion to exam = SMA embolus (past the proximal jejunal branches) → CTA → embolectomy + lifelong anticoagulation.
  • Thrombosis (~20%, origin plaque) → aortomesenteric bypass · NOMI (low flow, pressors) → bowel rest, fluids, antibiotics · SMV thrombosis → anticoagulate · dead bowel → resect. "When in doubt, operate."
  • Chronic: middle-aged woman, postprandial pain, weight loss (food fear) → CTA → revascularise.
  • Collaterals: pancreaticoduodenal arcade (celiac–SMA), marginal artery (SMA–IMA); watershed = splenic flexure, rectosigmoid.
