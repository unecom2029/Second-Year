Lipid Drugs

  • Statin → HMG-CoA reductase → ↑ LDL receptors. High intensity: atorva, rosuva (post-MI). Non-CYP: prava, rosuva. CYP3A4: lova, simva, atorva.
  • Ezetimibe (NPC1L1): add-on; weak alone (synthesis ↑). Sequestrants: GI, vit K deficiency, ↑TG, bind drugs. Fibrates (PPAR-α): ↓TG ↑HDL; myopathy, gallstones. Niacin: ↑HDL; flushing (aspirin), hyperglycemia, gout. Omega-3: ↓TG. PCSK9 mAb / inclisiran siRNA / oral: after max statin + ezetimibe.
  • DDIs: statin + CYP3A4 inhibitor (clarithromycin, azoles, PIs, grapefruit, diltiazem/verapamil) → rhabdo · statin + gemfibrozil → rhabdo (use fenofibrate) · fibrate + warfarin → protein-binding displacement, bleeding · sequestrant + other drugs → space doses.
  • Prevent: don't front-load myalgia counselling (nocebo); tea-coloured urine → CK; switch statin/dose; aspirin before niacin; no statins in pregnancy.
