Dyslipidemias

  • Chylomicron (gut, B-48, dietary TG) · VLDL (liver, B-100, TG) → IDL → LDL (B-100, cholesterol, cleared by hepatic LDL receptor) · HDL (A-I, LCAT, reverse transport).
  • PCSK9 degrades LDL receptors; loss-of-function → low LDL, less CVD.
  • Type I (AR, LPL/apoC-II): chylomicrons, pancreatitis, eruptive xanthomas, no ↑ atherosclerosis · Type II FH (AD, LDL receptor/apoB-100): LDL, tendon xanthomas, arcus <45, MI young · III (AR, apoE): remnants, palmar xanthomas · IV (AD, ↑VLDL): TG, pancreatitis.
  • Secondary ↑TG: diabetes, alcohol, CKD, estrogen, β-blockers, thiazides, steroids, HIV PIs. ↑Cholesterol: hypothyroidism, nephrotic, cholestasis, anorexia.
  • Diet ↓ cholesterol ~25%. ↑LDL: replace saturated fat, no trans fat, fibre. ↑TG: cut refined carbs, sugar, alcohol; fish. Mediterranean/DASH (Section 4).
