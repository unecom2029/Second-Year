Cardiomyopathies

  • Dilated (EF <40%, systolic): genetic (TTN), alcohol, peripartum, myocarditis (Coxsackie B, Chagas), hemochromatosis, doxorubicin, sarcoid. Treat as HFrEF + ICD.
  • Hypertrophic (1 in 500, AD, sarcomere MYH7/MYBPC3): asymmetric septal hypertrophy, SAM, myofibre disarray; murmur ↑ Valsalva; appears in adolescence. #1 cause of sudden death in young athletes (~36%). Do less, β-blocker, septal myectomy/alcohol ablation, ICD; avoid diuretics, nitrates, vasodilators.
  • Restrictive: amyloid (low-voltage ECG, thick walls, apple-green birefringence), radiation, sarcoid, hemochromatosis, Löffler. Big atria. Mimic: constrictive pericarditis.
  • ARVC: fibro-fatty RV, VT, epsilon wave; ICD.
  • Takotsubo: postmenopausal woman, stressor, apical ballooning, clean coronaries.
