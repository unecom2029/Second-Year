Heart Tumors

  • Myxoma: most common primary (adults), left atrium, stalk at fossa ovalis; ball-valve positional syncope/dyspnea, emboli (TIA), IL-6 fevers/weight loss, tumour plop; stellate cells in myxoid stroma. Resect stalk + septal cuff → no recurrence (Carney complex can recur).
  • Rhabdomyoma: most common in children, ventricles, tuberous sclerosis, often regress.
  • Papillary fibroelastoma: mitral chordae, emboli.
  • Metastases: most common overall (~10% of autopsies); bloody pericardial effusion → tamponade; RCC up the IVC.
