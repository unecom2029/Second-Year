Hypertension

  • Stage 1 130–139/80–89 · stage 2 ≥140/≥90 · severe >180/120. Measure right (cuff over clothing ±50 mm Hg); home/ambulatory → white-coat vs masked.
  • 90–95% essential (salt, genes). Pump (young, ↑CO) vs vascular (old, ↑SVR). Damage: hyaline arteriolosclerosis, LVH, stroke, nephrosclerosis, retinopathy; malignant = onion skin.
  • Look for secondary: resistant, abrupt, <30, TOD out of proportion, malignant, new diastolic ≥65, unprovoked ↓K⁺.
  • Causes: OSA (>30% of resistant) · PA (↓K, alkalosis, ↓renin, ↑ARR, no edema, polyuria) · renal artery stenosis (bruit, flash edema, ↑renin ↑aldo; FMD in young women) · renal parenchymal · thyroid · Cushing · pheo (spells, metanephrines) · coarct · pre-eclampsia · drugs (NSAIDs, decongestants, OCPs, steroids, calcineurin inhibitors, cocaine).
  • Renin/aldo: ↓/↑ PA · ↑/↑ secondary · ↓/↓ Liddle, licorice, Cushing, CAH.
  • PA: 60% bilateral hyperplasia (MRA) vs 30% adenoma (CT + AVS → adrenalectomy). ARR only valid with suppressed renin; stop MRA 4 wk; β-blocker false +.
  • Resistant = 3 drugs incl. diuretic. Exclude pseudo-resistance; add spironolactone.
  • Urgency (no TOD) → oral, slow. Emergency (TOD) → IV, ↓MAP ≤25% in hour 1 (dissection: SBP <120 fast). Cushing reflex: ↑SBP + bradycardia → raised ICP.
