Heart Failure Drugs

  • Mortality ↓: ARNI (> ACEi > ARB), β-blocker (carvedilol, metoprolol succinate, bisoprolol), MRA, SGLT2i (also HFpEF); hydralazine + ISDN add-on (A-HeFT). Symptoms only: diuretics, digoxin, nitrates, inotropes, tolvaptan.
  • All four: HR 0.84 → 0.36; ~5 years longer life. Start low doses of all four in hospital, then titrate over 4–6 weeks.
  • Sacubitril inhibits neprilysin → ↑ natriuretic peptides, bradykinin, substance P. 36-h ACEi washout (angioedema). Hypotension, ↑K⁺, fetal toxicity. Follow NT-proBNP.
  • β-blocker only when compensated. Carvedilol adds α1 block (↓ afterload).
  • Spironolactone: ↑K⁺, gynecomastia (antiandrogen) → eplerenone. ACEi/ARNI + MRA → hyperkalemia.
  • SGLT2i: natriuresis, tubuloglomerular feedback → afferent constriction; Fournier gangrene, euglycemic DKA, genital infection.
  • Furosemide: NKCC2; ↓K/Na/Ca/Mg, hypokalemic alkalosis, ototoxicity; sulfa allergy → ethacrynic acid. Braking (DCT hypertrophy) → add thiazide.
  • Dobutamine = β1 (racemic, α cancels). Milrinone = PDE3. Norepinephrine → reflex bradycardia. Nitroprusside = spontaneous NO, arteries + veins, cyanide. Ivabradine = If. Vericiguat = sGC stimulator.
  • Starling: preload ↓ slides down the same curve; afterload ↓ or inotrope moves to a higher curve.
