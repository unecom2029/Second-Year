Endocarditis: Who and What

  • Acute: S. aureus, normal valve, fulminant, broad (MRSA) cover. Subacute: viridans strep, damaged valve, dental source. Now ~75% acute.
  • In-hospital mortality 15–20%, 1-year up to 40%; ~half need valve surgery.
  • Risk: >50 y, men, dental disease, structural disease (75%; MVP with MR now #1 over RHD), devices (23%), IDU, dialysis, diabetes.
  • Pathogenesis: endothelial injury → sterile platelet–fibrin nidus → bacteremia → colonisation → biofilm (~500× resistant, slow-growing bacteria).
  • Vegetation → seeding, valve destruction (perforation, ring abscess, block), septic emboli (stroke, mycotic aneurysm, spleen/kidney infarcts).
  • Left: mitral > aortic; systemic emboli. Right: tricuspid (~60% in IDU), S. aureus 70–80%, no murmur, septic pulmonary emboli; repair tricuspid first.
  • Organisms: S. aureus 31% (GPC, catalase+, coagulase+) · viridans 17% · S. epidermidis 11% = prosthetic valves · enterococci 11% (urinary catheters) · S. gallolyticus → colon cancer · HACEK · fungi (bulky, immunocompromised).
  • Culture-negative: antibiotics before cultures (commonest) · Coxiella (Q fever, phase I IgG >1:800) · Bartonella (cats, homeless). Libman–Sacks (SLE) and marantic = sterile.
