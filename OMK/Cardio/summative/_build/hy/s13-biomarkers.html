Troponin & BNP

  • Troponin = myocytes dying. hs-cTn rises 1–3 h, peaks 12–24 h, elevated 7–10 d. Diagnose MI by rise and/or fall on serial values (repeat at 1–3 h). Height ∝ infarct size.
  • Specific for heart muscle, not for infarction: marathon, PE, myocarditis, chronic HF, sepsis, CKD (flat), takotsubo.
  • CK-MB back to normal in 2–3 d → re-infarction.
  • BNP = ventricular stretch. Pro-BNP → active BNP (32 aa, t½ ~20 min) + inert NT-proBNP (76 aa, t½ 1–2 h). Acts via cGMP: vasodilation, natriuresis, ↓ renin/aldosterone.
  • Rule-out: BNP ≤100, NT-proBNP ≤300. Rule-in: BNP >400; NT-proBNP >450 / 900 / 1,800 by age (<50 / 50–75 / >75).
  • Sacubitril ↑ BNP (neprilysin blocked) → follow NT-proBNP. CKD ↑ both (NT-proBNP more). Obesity falsely lowers both. Age ↑.
