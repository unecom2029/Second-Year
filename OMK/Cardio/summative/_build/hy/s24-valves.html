Valvular Heart Disease

  • Left side matters. Stenosis = pressure load (concentric); regurgitation = volume load (eccentric). AV valves can be repaired; semilunar valves usually replaced.
  • AS: bicuspid (40s–60s; screen for ascending aneurysm + coarctation) vs calcific (70s+; lamellar bone; statins don't help). Symptoms → ~50% dead in 2 yr. No drug slows it → replace. Heyde syndrome (vWF cleaved → GI bleed). Avoid nitrates.
  • AR: root (Marfan, syphilis, dissection, ankylosing spondylitis) or valve (endocarditis, bicuspid). Surgery: symptoms, EF <50%, ESD >55 mm. No TAVR in pure AR.
  • MS: rheumatic, fish-mouth fused commissures; AF + LA thrombus (warfarin). β-blocker, diuretic, balloon commissurotomy, MVR (tissue in young women).
  • MR: MVP (myxomatous) commonest US cause; functional from dilated LV or inferior MI. Organic → repair, functional → replace.
  • Upstream cascade AR → MR → pulmonary HTN → TR → right failure: fix valves early.
  • RHD: GAS M-protein mimicry (~3%), Aschoff bodies, Anitschkow cells; mitral > aortic > tricuspid > pulmonic.
  • Right side: TR mostly functional (left HF); IDU, carcinoid (TR + PS), leads. Tricuspid: repair first; tissue valve if replaced. PS congenital (Noonan) → balloon. PR after tetralogy repair.
