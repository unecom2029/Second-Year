Myocarditis & Pericarditis

  • Myocarditis: young (20–50), viral prodrome then unexplained HF/chest pain/arrhythmia; troponin up (plateau). Viruses: parvo B19, HHV-6 now; Coxsackie B classic (CAR receptor with adenovirus). Diphtheria toxin; Lyme → heart block (~10%); Chagas (kissing bug, cardiomyopathy + megacolon/megaesophagus, now US-endemic); checkpoint inhibitors.
  • Histology: lymphocytic = viral · eosinophilic = parasite/hypersensitivity · giant-cell · granulomatous = sarcoid. EMB definitive. Treatment supportive, bed rest/no exertion, antivirals unproven.
  • Pericarditis: sharp pleuritic pain, better leaning forward, radiates to trapezius, friction rub, diffuse STE + PR depression (no territory). ≥2 of 4: pain, rub, ECG, effusion. NSAIDs + colchicine.
  • Causes: viral (Coxsackie, parvo, CMV), purulent (S. aureus, pneumococcus), TB, uremia, post-MI/Dressler, SLE/RA, cancer, radiation. Fluid types: serous · fibrinous ("bread and butter") · purulent · hemorrhagic (cancer) · constrictive.
  • ECG stages: STE/PR↓ → normal → diffuse TWI (wk 2–4) → normal.
  • Tamponade: Beck triad (hypotension, JVD, muffled sounds) + pulsus paradoxus + electrical alternans; water-bottle CXR; pericardiocentesis or subxiphoid window.
  • Constrictive: Kussmaul sign, pericardial knock; mimics restrictive cardiomyopathy.
