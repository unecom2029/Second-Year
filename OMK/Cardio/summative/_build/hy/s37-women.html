Cardiovascular health in women

  • CVD = 1 in 3 deaths in US women; >10,000 MIs/yr under 45; under 65, MI mortality ~2× men's. Age is a risk factor from >50 (men >40).
  • Atypical symptoms; SCAD, Takotsubo, MINOCA, microvascular disease cluster in women.
  • Enhancers: menarche ≤10 (RR 1.27, U-shaped), preeclampsia, gestational HTN/diabetes, pregnancy loss, preterm birth; postmenopause weighted like male sex.
  • MHT: WHI/HERS no benefit (women >10 yr post-menopause); timing hypothesis — start <10 yr after menopause and <60 · avoid with ASCVD, VTE, breast cancer, 10-yr risk ≥7.5%.
