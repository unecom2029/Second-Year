Vasculitis

  • Mechanisms: immune complex (HSP-IgA, cryo-HCV, PAN-HBV, SLE) · ANCA pauci-immune (GPA PR3/c, MPA MPO/p, EGPA) · anti-GBM · cell-mediated granulomatous (GCA, Takayasu) · infection/mimicry (syphilis, Cogan, Buerger-tobacco).
  • Large: GCA (>50, headache, jaw claudication, PMR, ↑ESR, granulomas + fragmented IEL; steroids before biopsy — blindness) · Takayasu (young Asian women, pulseless, unequal arm BP, angiography, steroids).
  • Medium: PAN (HBV, ANCA−, spares lung, fibrinoid necrosis, renal/GI/nerve) · Kawasaki (<5, CRASH + fever ≥5 d, ↑platelets, coronary aneurysm → echo, IVIG + aspirin) · Buerger (young male smokers, distal ischemia, corkscrew collaterals, arteries + veins, stop smoking).
  • Small: GPA (sinus + lung + kidney, c-ANCA, granulomas) · MPA (lung + kidney, p-ANCA, no granulomas) · EGPA (asthma, eosinophils, neuropathy) · HSP (child after URI: purpura, arthritis, abdominal pain, GN) · cryo (HCV, cold) · anti-GBM (lung hemorrhage + GN).
  • Behçet: HLA-B51, Turkey/Middle East, oral + genital ulcers, uveitis. Leukocytoclastic = nuclear dust.
  • Severe ANCA disease: steroids + rituximab/cyclophosphamide ± plasma exchange.
