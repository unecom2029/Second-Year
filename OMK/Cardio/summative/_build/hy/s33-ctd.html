Connective Tissue Disorders

  • Marfan: FBN1 fibrillin-1, AD; root dilation → AR, type A dissection (lifetime up to 30%), MVP >90%; tall, arachnodactyly, upward lens dislocation, pectus.
  • Vascular EDS: COL3A1 collagen III, AD; rupture of medium arteries, bowel, uterus; thin translucent skin; avoid invasive procedures.
  • Loeys–Dietz: TGF-β receptor; tortuous arteries, bifid uvula, hypertelorism; dissects at smaller sizes → earlier surgery.
  • Turner (45,X): coarctation 25–30%, BAV, aortic dissection (esp. pregnancy).
  • Manage: serial imaging, β-blocker ± losartan, prophylactic root replacement (~5.0 cm Marfan, smaller in LDS), avoid contact sports, plan pregnancy.
