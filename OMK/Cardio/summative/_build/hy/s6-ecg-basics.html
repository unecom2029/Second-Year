Reading the ECG

  • Same order every time: rate → rhythm → axis → intervals (PR, QRS, QT) → chamber enlargement → ischemia → one sentence.
  • Rate = QRS on the strip × 6 (12-lead = 10 s; 6-s strip × 10). Tachy ≥100, brady <60.
  • Small box 40 ms · big box 200 ms · PR 120–200 · QRS <120 · QTc <450 M / <460 F, danger >500.
  • Sinus = 5 criteria: R–R regular, P–P regular, PR constant, a P for every QRS, P upright in II (biphasic in V1).
  • 5 irregular rhythms: AF (no P, irregularly irregular) · sinus arrhythmia (with breathing) · 2nd-degree AV block · WAP (≥3 P shapes, <100) · MAT (same, ≥100).
  • Flutter: sawtooth in II, III, aVF; fixed ratio (regular) or variable (irregular). Right atrium / cavotricuspid isthmus; AF = pulmonary veins / left atrium.
  • Axis from I and aVF: both up = normal · I up, aVF down = left · I down, aVF up = right. aVF isoelectric → use lead II.
  • Left axis: LVH, LBBB, LAFB, old inferior MI. Right axis: RVH, LPFB, old lateral MI.
  • RBBB has a normal axis — RBBB + right axis = RBBB + LPFB.
