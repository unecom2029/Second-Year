Congenital Heart Disease

  • 1 in 125 births, most common birth defect, first 8 weeks, ~90% unexplained. Blood goes high → low pressure; after the first breath PVR falls, so open holes shunt L→R.
  • Acyanotic (L→R, ↑ lung flow): VSD (commonest; holosystolic LLSB; small = louder; small ones close by 2 yr) · ASD (fixed split S2, AF, paradoxical embolism; Amplatzer) · PDA (machinery murmur, preterm, rubella; indomethacin closes, PGE₁ keeps open). Coarctation = obstruction.
  • Eisenmenger: long L→R shunt → fixed pulmonary HTN → R→L → late cyanosis, clubbing, polycythemia; too late to close.
  • Cyanotic — 5 T's: Truncus · Transposition · Tricuspid atresia · Tetralogy · TAPVR (+ HLHS, Ebstein).
  • TOF: VSD + RVOT stenosis + RVH + overriding aorta; stenosis sets cyanosis; tet spells (↓SVR) → knee-chest/squat (↑SVR); boot-shaped heart; BT shunt historically; 22q11.
  • TGA: aorta from RV; needs mixing; egg on a string; PGE₁ ± septostomy → arterial switch; maternal diabetes. HLHS: shock as ductus closes → PGE₁ → Norwood/Glenn/Fontan.
  • CXR: boot (TOF), egg on string (TGA), snowman (TAPVR), box (Ebstein), rib notching (coarct).
  • Syndromes: Down → AVSD · Turner → coarct + BAV · 22q11 → truncus, TOF · Williams → supravalvular AS · Noonan → PS · rubella → PDA · lithium → Ebstein · maternal diabetes → TGA · Marfan → MVP/root.
