MI Histology Timeline

  • 0–½ h: EM only (reversible) · ½–4 h: wavy fibres at border · 4–12 h: early coagulative necrosis, edema · 12–24 h: dark mottling, pyknosis, hypereosinophilia, contraction bands, first neutrophils.
  • 1–3 d: yellow-tan centre; loss of nuclei/striations, neutrophils.
  • 3–7 d: hyperemic border, soft; macrophages → softest wall = rupture window.
  • 7–10 d: maximally yellow and soft, granulation at margins · 10–14 d: granulation tissue, new vessels, early collagen · 2–8 wk: grey-white scar · >2 mo: dense scar.
  • Wavy-fibre timing differs (lecture ½–4 h, First Aid 0–4 h, UWorld 4–12 h): first LM change, day 1.
  • Transmural = complete occlusion (STEMI); subendocardial = inner third, partial occlusion (NSTEMI).
  • Reperfusion injury: Ca²⁺ floods in → contraction band necrosis; free radicals; hemorrhagic infarct.
