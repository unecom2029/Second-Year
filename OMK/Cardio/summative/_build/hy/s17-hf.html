HFrEF vs HFpEF

  • CHF ≈ 50% 5-year survival. Pressure overload → sarcomeres in parallel → concentric (HTN, AS). Volume overload → in series → eccentric (AR, MR, post-MI).
  • HFrEF (EF ≤40%): ↓ contractility, ESPVR flatter, ↑EDV/ESV, dilated, S3; younger man post-MI; CV death. HFpEF (EF ≥50%): ↓ compliance, steep EDPVR, ↓EDV at ↑EDP, EF preserved, S4; older woman, obesity, HTN, DM, CKD, AF; early endothelial dysfunction, stiff N2B titin.
  • Neurohumoral spiral: ↓ BP → sympathetic → β1 renin → AT II (↑ afterload) → aldosterone (↑ preload) → ↑ O₂ demand. Dilated chambers resist natriuretic peptide.
  • Left HF: dyspnea, orthopnea, PND, crackles, heart-failure cells. Right HF: ↑JVP, +HJR, edema, nutmeg liver → cardiac cirrhosis. Commonest cause of right HF = left HF.
  • High-output HF: AV fistula, thyrotoxicosis, anemia, wet beriberi (↑CO, ↓SVR). NYHA I–IV + ACC/AHA stage A–D.
  • Forrester: wet-warm commonest (diurese); wet-cold = cardiogenic shock (ICU, inotropes). Decongest preload first; no new β-blocker while decompensated.
  • Labs: CBC, CMP, TSH, A1c, iron, BNP/NT-proBNP; no office troponin. Echo diastolic grade 2 = ↑LA pressure. CXR Kerley B. H₂FPEF: heavy 2, HTN 1, AF 3, PASP 1, >60 1, E/e′ 1.
  • Cocaine + β1-selective blocker → unopposed α → ↑ afterload.
